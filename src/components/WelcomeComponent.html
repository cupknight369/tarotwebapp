Welcome

This tarot application is intended to act as a journal. It allows you to save readings with custom titles so that you can look back on them later. All of your cards and text will be saved in the exact location you left them in - no need to keep up with photos of card spreads or describe a physical reading through text (unless you want to).

You could even use this application to mirror a real-life reading that was preformed! It can act as a digital copy of a real meaningful moment in life.

There are no limits to how you can utilize this application - it was designed to be extremely flexible. You can even create your own custom decks with your own images.

All information is saved on your device locally. This app does not collect or store your information. You don't even need to create an account to use it.

The only reason you would need an account is if you want to purchase any custom decks from the cloud store. There you will find custom designed decks from other users of this marketplace. The minimum amount of data necessary to complete a transaction and store a record of your purchase will be collected for this purpose.

Quick Notes

  • Local saves: Readings, labels, and deck data stay on this device.
  • Storage remaining: Open `⚙` App Settings and check \"IndexedDB storage (estimate)\".
  • Low-storage warning: A warning appears on canvas when less than 20% storage remains.
  • Export/Import: In `⚙` App Settings, use export/import backup buttons.
  • Function descriptions: Open `?` Help for concise feature guidance.
