Help

Draw Cards

  • Purpose: Add cards from the active deck onto the canvas.
  • How to use: Use `1` for single draw, `N` for random multiple, and `#` for draw-by-index.

Canvas Navigation

  • Purpose: Move around and inspect large layouts.
  • How to use: Drag empty canvas space to pan, and use the mouse wheel or pinch gesture to zoom.

Context Menu and Selection

  • Purpose: Perform quick actions on cards, text, and canvas.
  • How to use: Right-click to open actions; hold Ctrl and drag to box-select cards for group operations.

Text Labels

  • Purpose: Annotate card layouts with custom notes.
  • How to use: Add text from canvas menu/shortcut, then drag, style, resize, and anchor labels to cards.

Deck Management

  • Purpose: Switch decks and manage deck content on-device.
  • How to use: Use `🎴` to open deck picker tabs, download/remove decks, create decks, or open deck editor.

Save, Load, and New Reading

  • Purpose: Persist your layout and move between reading sessions.
  • How to use: Use `💾` to save, `📂` to load/edit/delete saved readings, and `✨` to start a new reading.

Clear Canvas

  • Purpose: Remove all cards and text from the current canvas.
  • How to use: Click `🧹` and confirm to clear everything from the active session.

Settings and Backup

  • Purpose: Configure display options and safeguard local data.
  • How to use: Click `⚙` to toggle settings and run export/import backup workflows.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Purpose: Speed up common actions.
  • How to use: Use `Ctrl+S` save, `Ctrl+D` draw card, `Ctrl+N` draw by name, `Ctrl+T` add text, and `Esc` close open modals/dialogs.
