Reducing eviction risk

  • Keep free disk space on your device; browsers may reclaim storage when the system is low on space.
  • Avoid relying on private/incognito windows for data you need to keep—site data is often cleared when those sessions end.
  • In your browser settings, review site data usage for this site and remove other large or unused origins if you need more room.
  • Use this app periodically and export backups regularly so a cleanup does not catch you without a copy.
  • Where your browser offers “persistent storage” or similar for this site, enable it so the browser is less likely to treat your data as disposable.

Details vary by browser and device; see your browser’s help for storage and site data.
